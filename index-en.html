AI OUTPUT INSPECTION TOOL

Yomitoki

Re-measure what the AI told you.

A web tool that examines AI replies through eight categories, distinguishing fact, inference, and opinion while checking for unsupported assertions and excessive affirmation. It helps you reread the original for yourself by comparing it with the re-measured reply.

  • Fact
  • Inference
  • Opinion
  • Encouragement
  • Unsupported assertion
  • Excessive affirmation
  • Overconfident prediction
  • Unsourced citation

Concept & testing | SAORI (TAKIBI lab)

I define the themes, design the experiences, and set the evaluation criteria, then test on real devices. I put what feels off into words and keep refining each work through completion.

Development | With AI partners

I work with AI partners on code, images, sound, and motion, with final decisions made by a human. Sometimes I develop the same concept with different models to compare and evaluate its expression and behavior.
